Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I hear back from you?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly.

Can I book a trip directly online?

You can use our trip calculator to get an estimate and request an appointment for a consultation. We'll finalize bookings with you personally to ensure all details are perfect.

What if I need to cancel or modify my booking?

We understand that plans change. Contact us directly to discuss cancellation policies specific to your booking and dates.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes, we can advise on and help arrange travel insurance options suitable for your trip and requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, bank transfers, and other payment methods. Discuss your preference with our consultants.

Can I customize a package from your website?

Absolutely! Any of our packages can be customized. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements and preferences.
